**原始材料。** 旧版上月上传通过 90/100，本月 76/80。旧版包含移动端和桌面端，本月只测桌面端。试用收到 11 条反馈，其中 6 条提到失败后要重新填写。新版修复后用 5 条桌面失败用例验证，5 条都恢复了已填内容。移动端补测仍待安排。目标听众需要决定是否扩大试点。

**一页稿。**

> **桌面端恢复路径已验证，扩大试点前补测移动端**
>
> 本轮桌面端上传用例通过 **76/80（95%）**。本次覆盖范围与上月不同。
>
> **修了什么**　失败后恢复已填内容。修复后回归 **5/5** 条桌面失败用例。
>
> **请决定**　先保持当前试点范围。QA 补测移动端上传和失败恢复，结果出齐后再决定扩大范围。

**口述。** 试用收到 11 条反馈，6 条提到重新填写；这是问题线索。上月 90% 覆盖两个端，与本月 95% 不适合直接计算提升。详尽的设备覆盖和逐条用例放附录。

Six of 11 pilot feedback entries mentioned re-entering information after failure. After a fix, all five desktop failure regression cases recovered the entered information. Mobile retesting is pending. The audience must decide whether to expand the pilot.

**One-page version.**

> **Desktop recovery verified. Retest mobile before expanding the pilot.**
>
> **76/80 desktop upload cases passed (95%)** this round, on a different test scope from last month.
>
> **Verified improvement** — Entered information recovered in **5/5** desktop failure regression cases after the fix.
>
> **Decision requested** — Hold the current pilot size. Have QA retest mobile upload and failure recovery, then revisit expansion.

**Speaker notes.** Six of 11 feedback entries flagged re-entry. Treat feedback as leads, not a measured rate. Device coverage changed, so a percentage-point difference would misrepresent the evidence. Keep detailed case coverage in the appendix.
